## What this company is

- Clinical-stage biotech, founded on single-cell analysis of the tumor microenvironment.
- Founding science came from Dana-Farber Cancer Institute, Mass General Cancer Center, the Broad
  Institute and MIT; the team identified CD161 (KLRB1) as a T-cell target, published in Cell (2021).
- Lead program IMT-009, an anti-CD161 antibody, is in a Phase 1/2a dose-escalation and expansion
  study in advanced solid tumors and hematological malignancies, including a Phase 1b combination
  with fruquintinib in microsatellite-stable colorectal cancer.
- A second, preclinical anti-CD161 program (IMT-380) for chronic inflammatory / autoimmune disease
  was licensed to Boehringer Ingelheim in May 2026.
- Completed a $58M Series B in August 2021.
